    How it works. The slide is a 120 × 80 grid where one step is 50 µm and one tick is 6 hours. Each tumor cell with free space next to it divides with the chosen growth rate. Cells with no free neighbours for a long time die, which forms the pale core. Edge cells sometimes crawl away (invasiveness). Cells touching the vessel can enter the blood; one in four that reach the end of the vessel forms a colony. Each division has a 0.3% chance of producing a drug-resistant cell. Chemotherapy kills dividing, non-resistant cells.

    This is an illustration with made-up rates, not a clinical model.
